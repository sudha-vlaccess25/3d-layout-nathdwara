Layout principles

  • Each primary server and its failover partner sit in different racks (R2 and R3), so one rack fault cannot take down both.
  • Storage enclosures are at the bottom of R5 for weight; GPU servers are grouped in R4 for cooling.
  • Firewalls, core and distribution switches are consolidated in R1 at the head of the row.
  • R6 to R8 are kept free for future cameras, sensors and storage.
  • Rack fronts face the cold aisle with perforated tiles; UPS and battery banks are on the rear wall; precision AC runs N+1.

Device heights (2U servers, 4U LiDAR server, 4U storage shelves) are assumed for layout; confirm against final OEM models. Screen content on the video wall is illustrative.
